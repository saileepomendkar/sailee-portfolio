Areas of Practice

Core Clinical Competencies

Neurological Rehabilitation (Pediatric & Adult)

Targeted neurodevelopmental and motor control interventions tailored for pediatric conditions and adult neurological recovery.

Bobath Approach

Specialized clinical handling and postural control strategies aimed at optimizing movement efficiency in hemiplegia and central nervous system disorders.

Neurodynamic Solutions

Upper and lower limb neural mobilization techniques to alleviate nerve root irritation and restore healthy peripheral nerve mechanics.

Manual Therapy Techniques

Hands-on joint mobilization, soft tissue release, and integrated manual techniques to reduce pain and restore joint kinematics.

Clinical Assessment & Evaluation

Comprehensive evaluation of pain, range of motion, muscle strength, posture, and functional mobility to establish clear recovery baselines.

Postsurgical Rehabilitation

Structured inpatient early mobilization, respiratory care, and progressive outpatient strengthening following orthopedic and medical surgeries.

Antenatal & Postnatal Physiotherapy

Safe, certified exercise prescription supporting maternal musculoskeletal health, core stability, and postpartum recovery.

Patient Education & Counseling

Clear posture correction, ergonomic guidance, and tailored home exercise programmes empowering long-term self-management.

Evidence-Based Practice

Integrating current clinical research with measurable outcome tracking across multi-week rehabilitation cycles.
